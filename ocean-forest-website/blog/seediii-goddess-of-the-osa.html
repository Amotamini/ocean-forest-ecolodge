See~diii: a 9th dimensional goddess protects the Osa Peninsula

In 2008 a group of Taoist masters asked to be taken to Caño Island, the small island you can see from our beach. Jonathon Miller Weisberger, who has stewarded this lodge since 2000, went with them. This is his account of what they said they found there.

Her name is See~diii, she is a goddess from the 9th dimension, she protects the Osa Peninsula and the phenomena of Heaven on Earth, and she can be prayed to.

That is what our good hearted masters of Tao whispered softly to us.

It was April 2008. My good friend Donna Runnalls of the Living Bridges Foundation, a non-profit devoted to the preservation of Indigenous life ways that I also sit on the board of, had been organising visits to the United States for a group of Taoist masters. On one of those visits they asked her whether she knew anything about Caño Island, off the coast of Costa Rica. The island is considered to be a celestial inter-dimensional portal. She told them, enthusiastically, that she had a friend who ran an ecolodge right there, and they encouraged her to get in touch with me. It was not long before they were booked in at Ocean Forest, then known as Guaria de Osa, and I was showing them some of the sacred sites of southern Costa Rica.

It was a high honour to guide these noble masters, whose devotion to teaching ancient spiritual arts in the service of all life is both humbling and inspiring. I went with don Memo, my old friend and a traditional Brunka elder.

El Silencio, and the ridge of dolls

We went first to El Silencio, a place known ancestrally as Shwing'tang, where you find a stone sphere two and a half metres across, the largest of all the petro-spheres of southern Costa Rica. Memito, as we fondly call him, told us that when he was young this whole area was wild. Animals were everywhere. The valley was completely intact. Any small creek held jumbo shrimp and crabs and many kinds of fish, and birds of every sort lived there in abundance.

The ridge behind the sphere is known as the ridge of dolls, where dozens of stone statues of many sizes once stood. Some of them can be seen today in the National Museum in San José. None remain on the ridge. Apart from the steep mountain valleys, the rainforest there has been cleared for cattle and timber.

A small hut of sticks with a half fallen plastic roof stood in the parched landscape. As we came close the masters asked us to wait at the car, and their secretary and interpreter Mary Ho asked us kindly to stay still and quiet. For about an hour and a half they sat in that hut meditating. We were not far off. I could hear them whispering gently, and at times chanting softly, and it was soothing to listen to, as though they were deep in conversation with a close friend.

They had received a visitation, they told us afterwards, from an ancient goddess, the spiritual guardian of that area. As we had passed the hut she had been sitting there waiting to speak with them.

The goddess told them that to know more they must go to Caño Island, where a goddess of an even higher realm was waiting to meet them. She said that they had wanted to communicate with representatives of the human realm for some time, and that there were a few people in the region who could perceive her, but none who could see her and speak with her. When she saw that the masters had arrived, she took her opportunity.

In old times, Memito told us, there was a woman named Olla who lived among the people. Although she was very old she always looked young. She would arrive at the festivals and animate everyone in song and dance, and then vanish as mysteriously as she had come.

The Floating Butterfly

A few days later we arrived at Caño Island, a place known ancestrally as the Floating Butterfly, and walked up to the centre of the island where the trail leads to an archaeological site. Caño Island was a burial place for the ancient royalty, for skilled navigators and spiritual masters, for the outstanding people of those times. A fisherman friend told me that when he visited in 1969 there was a ring of life sized statues carved in stone, with several granite spheres of various sizes set among them. That site has been looted. None of it remains.

Master Tianying began softly chanting. Time suspended itself for a moment and was replaced by another kind of time, where creativity is boundless, gravity seems to loosen its grip, and everything gathers into the presence of the immediate now.

While meditating, the masters received a visitation from a goddess whose name, they said, is See~diii.

The island's true name is the Island of the Spheres of the Giants. See~diii comes from the 9th dimension and governs the protection of the Osa Peninsula. There are malicious spirits trying to make the Osa Peninsula sink into the water, and See~diii is working diligently with multitudes of heavenly beings to prevent it. The most important thing is to protect the ocean and the rainforest.

Her main concerns, they said, are protecting great nature, reversing its destruction, and helping people recover their inner harmony. The masters were very glad to learn that Caño Island and Corcovado are now protected areas, and they applauded the efforts made to protect these living terrestrial treasures.

The spheres, and the giants

Grandmaster Da Shi Le Tian went on to say that the goddess had told them it was she herself who brought the first spheres to this land. Mary Ho continued to interpret, the masters speaking only in Mandarin.

See~diii had brought the spheres from the 9th dimension approximately fifty thousand years ago, in order to help the giants who had been displaced by the sinking of the ancient motherland continent of Mu, and who had gathered here on the island. With the aid of the spheres she was able to assist them in their ascension, helping them transform into higher dimensional realms. Given the integrity of the oaths they had made, some were able to ascend to the heavenly realms, and others were transformed into the fourth dimensional realm, which is the realm inside the water and inside the earth.

She spoke too of a race of sea bearing people who lived along the shores of the mainland across from the island. About a thousand years ago she came to them and helped them transform into the water realm, at a time when they were still pure enough to accomplish it. She revealed to them the prophecy of the coming age of confusion, and they willingly followed her and entrusted themselves to her. They live there now in beautiful underwater temples, as immortals of the inner dimension of the water realm.

What they carried away

The masters were very happy with their visit to the Island of the Spheres of the Giants, the Floating Butterfly. It gave them far more than they had expected. They explained that a great universal integration of spirits is taking place, and that they want to help humanity save the earth and itself. Mary Ho said that a spiritual encounter of this stature is rare, unique and extremely important.

They documented it extensively in writing. I glanced over at Master Tianying, who was busily writing in graceful Chinese calligraphy. She paused, looked at me and smiled. Her warm, reassuring smile carried a deeply felt peace, a motherly love for humanity and for the earth as deep and as vast as the ocean in front of us.

That peace I feel and carry with me to this day.
