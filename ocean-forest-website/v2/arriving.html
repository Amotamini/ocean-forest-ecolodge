Getting Here

Arriving Is Part of the Adventure

Every route to Ocean Forest Ecolodge ends the same way: a walk on the sand. Here is exactly what to expect, in the order you will need it.

Where This Is

Costa Rica, Osa Peninsula

Costa Rica, San Jose to the Osa Peninsula. Ocean Forest Ecolodge sits just south of Drake Bay, on the northern edge of Corcovado National Park.

The boat from Sierpe is the safest arrival and you do not need a car here.

Driving means a 4x4 and two river crossings, and in rain neither direction can be guaranteed.

Every route ends with a 20 minute walk on sand. Pack light, bring a backpack. Porter service exists and suitcases are fine, but nobody should discover this on arrival.

How You Get Here

Four Routes, One Beach

Boat from Sierpe → you land at San Josecito Beach → walk SOUTH 20 minutes, ocean on your right.

Donde Jorge Restaurant, Sierpe. Departs 11:30 AM ($30) or 4:00 PM ($40). Arrive 30 minutes early, 15 kg limit. 90 minutes through the mangroves and out to the Pacific. Lands at San Josecito Beach, walk south.

Boat from Sierpe → you land at San Josecito Beach → walk SOUTH 20 minutes, ocean on your right.

Tracopa bus from San Jose. Get off at Palmar Norte. Taxi to Sierpe. Connect with the afternoon boat.

Car, taxi or plane → you are dropped at San Josecito School → walk NORTH 20 minutes, ocean on your left.

4x4 required, two river crossings. About 370 km from San Jose. Park at the rural school. Walk north.

Car, taxi or plane → you are dropped at San Josecito School → walk NORTH 20 minutes, ocean on your left.

Sansa Airlines from San Jose, book a month ahead. 4x4 taxi transfer. Dropped at the school. Walk north.

Landing

What Happens When You Land

Every route ends the same way: a 20 minute walk along San Josecito Beach, in the direction your route card told you. If you asked for porter service, our staff will meet you at the drop point and help with your bags.

Departure Day

Going Home

You can either return the same way you came, or explore an alternate travel route. Here is a recap of the pertinent details from arrival, to help you plan your departure.

Via Drake Bay

  • 6:30 AMBreakfast
  • 7:00 AM20 minute beach walk south to the schoolhouse
  • 7:30 AMTaxi pickup, 45 minutes to Drake Bay Airport
  • 8:15 AMArrival at Drake Bay Airport
  • 9:10 AMFlight to San Jose, 50 minutes to Juan Santamaria International (SJO)

The quickest way back to San Jose, and the best option for connecting with an afternoon international flight. Give yourself at least 3 hours between arriving at the Domestic Terminal and your international departure.

Via the Sierpe River

  • 6:00 AMBreakfast
  • 6:30 AM20 minute beach walk north to San Josecito Bay
  • 6:50 AMWater ferry, 1.5 hours to Sierpe, through the largest mangrove estuary in the new world tropics
  • 8:30 AMArrival at the Sierpe river-town boat dock

Onward by land: a shuttle departs Sierpe around 10 AM; give yourself 5.5+ hours to San Jose International Airport. Onward by air: a 20 minute taxi to Palmar Sur airport connects with a 10:15 AM local flight, arriving SJO's Domestic Terminal around 11 AM. Transfer rates: $20 per person for the boat to Sierpe, then $5 per person in a shared taxi colectivo or $20 for a private taxi. Once your stay is booked, we reserve your local taxi and water shuttle transfers for you; please pay the service providers directly, as transfer fees are not included in our rates.

Travel Tips

What To Pack

What to pack

  • Head lamp or flashlight
  • Sunhat, beach towel and flip flops
  • Suntan lotion, sunscreen and bug repellant
  • Day pack and water bottle
  • Bathing suit and lightweight quick-dry clothes
  • Toothpaste, toothbrush, biodegradable toiletries
  • Portable fold-up umbrella or lightweight rain poncho, May through December
  • Pullover or light sweater for San Jose
  • Hiking shoes

Optional items

  • Sunglasses
  • Water shoes for getting on and off boats
  • Lightweight windbreaker or long sleeve cotton shirt for boat rides
  • Camera / binoculars
  • Snorkel, mask and flippers
  • Journal, coloring pens and pencils

Local flights allow 25 lbs; each pound overweight is charged $1 per pound. Remember the last leg of the journey is by foot, best to use a back pack rather than a suitcase. Non the less, porter service is provided, and suitcase is fine if it's what you have.
