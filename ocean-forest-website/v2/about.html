The story, the people, and the ground it stands on.

A note from the lodge

We built this place slowly, the way the forest builds itself.

A cabin first, then a path, then a garden, then a kitchen. Over many seasons it became a small community that learned how to live here without needing very much.

You will find ten rooms scattered into the trees, a three-storey wooden shala on the beach, and the people who make breakfast and walk the turtle patrols. Some of you come alone, to rest. Some of you arrive with thirty students and a programme to teach. All of you will leave with a memorable experience.

Ocean Forest Ecolodge Retreat is a rainforest and ocean discovery center and nature sanctuary located on a secluded mile-long beach south of Drake Bay, near Corcovado National Park on Costa Rica's Osa Peninsula. Situated on a 15-acre permaculture design plumb on the beach, the garden encompasses fruit and food orchards, ethnobotanical teas and aromatic herb gardens all that produce a seasonal cornucopia of delectable tropical foods, an abundance of aromatic herbs and marvelous medicinal plants.

An outstanding landmark of the Ecolodge is the Lapa Lapa Lodge, a magnificent three storied edifice resembling a pagoda. The building rises an impressive 14 meters or 46 feet above ground level and its tiered roof eaves invoke a sense of the spirit of humanity living in harmony with nature. Remarkably so, no living trees were cut for its edification as the entire building was constructed from sustainably sourced materials such as reclaimed hardwoods, old fallen timbers, bamboo and recycled products.

History

A beach with no road

Ocean Forest Ecolodge opened in 2003, on a beach with no road. A cabin came first, then a garden, then a kitchen, then the three-storey Lapa Lapa Lodge, built without cutting a single living tree.

Projects

The garden, the labyrinth, the timber

The lodge sits on a 15-acre permaculture design set plumb on the beach. Its gardens hold fruit and food orchards, ethnobotanical teas, and aromatic herb beds, a living ethnobotanical collection of medicinal and aromatic plants that also feeds the kitchen through the seasons.

Near the shore is the Turtle Island Labyrinth. Walking it is used as a meditation, and it is dedicated to the awareness of endangered marine sea turtles and to learning to live in harmony with all species of life on the planet.

The buildings were raised the same way the land is tended. The three-storey Lapa Lapa Lodge was constructed without cutting a single living tree, from sustainably sourced materials: reclaimed hardwoods, old fallen timbers, bamboo, and recycled products.

Recommendations

Before you set off

Before you travel

  • Make sure your passport is valid for at least 6 months beyond your departure, and carry proof of onward travel.
  • Travel insurance is recommended for cancellations and medical emergencies. Providers include Travel Guard, DAN, TravelInsurance.com, and Generali.
  • No vaccinations are required, unless you are arriving from specific African or South American countries, in which case a yellow fever certificate is needed, issued at least 10 days before entry.

Arriving in Costa Rica

  • Allow 2.5 to 3 hours between your international arrival at San José (SJO) and any domestic flight onward.
  • San José is subtropical and can be chilly at night, so pack a light sweater.

Money

  • Costa Rican Colones and US Dollars are both accepted.
  • There are no ATMs near Ocean Forest, Sierpe, or Drake Bay, so bring enough cash. ATMs are available in San José, Quepos, Dominical, and Palmar Norte.
  • Services at the lodge can be paid online through PayPal.
