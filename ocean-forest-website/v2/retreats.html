The premium thing

A beachfront sanctuary. Built for practice.

A 46-foot, three-level pagoda built entirely from reclaimed local hardwoods. It sits right on the beach. You practice to the sound of the ocean. The jungle starts where the property ends.

Video to come media/video/shala-360.mp4 waiting for: shala-360.mp4, Ryan · full 360° tour of the three floors

The ground floor, what we call the Dharma Hall, is 1,614 square feet of tiled space, always cool even in peak heat. Good for workshops, gatherings, and ceremony. The second floor is what teachers remember: smooth polished hardwood, full of light, fully equipped with props. It has its own name here: "foot candy."

The entire 3-level pagoda is yours for the duration of your retreat. No other groups sharing the space. Full yoga props included.

In 2003 we transformed an old horse pasture into a yoga shala, right on the beach. What started as a single structure became something larger, a fully self-contained ecolodge surrounded by ethnobotanical gardens, permaculture grounds, and one of the most biodiverse forest corridors on the planet.

We've spent over two decades refining what it means to host a retreat well. That means trained staff who stay out of your way until you need them, delicious food made by local chefs with ingredients sourced within an hour of the lodge, and a physical space that does real work on your students before class even starts.

"We've devoted much of our income to rainforest and oceanic wildlife conservation. When you bring your group here, that's part of what they're supporting too."

Before you write

Is Ocean Forest right for your group?

  • You want your students fully immersed in nature, with no city distractions, no competing noise
  • You're looking for a venue that handles logistics so you can focus entirely on teaching
  • Your group is between 8 and 32 people
  • You want 3 to 10 days of dedicated retreat time
  • Yoga, movement, dance, ceremony, workshops, or any combination. You bring the program, we provide the space
  • You value sustainability, since our income goes directly back into rainforest conservation
  • Your participants are ready for an experience that genuinely changes them

Worth knowing

We're remote by design. A 50-minute flight from San José, or a day's travel by road and boat. That remoteness is exactly what makes transformation possible here. But it does require participants who are comfortable with a real journey to get here. If your group needs easy accessibility or urban amenities nearby, we're probably not the right fit.
