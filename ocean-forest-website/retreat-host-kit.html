Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know
Before You Decide

Is Wi-Fi available?
Basic connectivity available for emergencies. We intentionally limit it — digital detox is part of the transformation.
What is the minimum group size?
We work with groups from 8 to 30 participants. Smaller groups may apply for partial-use arrangements.
Do I manage payments from participants?
Yes. You invoice your participants directly. We invoice you separately for the lodge package.
What if a participant needs to leave early?
Our team coordinates transfers to the airstrip or port. We handle it so you don't have to.
Can I customize the schedule?
100%. The schedule is yours. We adapt meals, activities, and logistics to your flow.
What is the cancellation policy?
Flexible terms for advance bookings. Full details provided in your personalized proposal.
Is it suitable for beginners?
Absolutely. The environment works for all levels — the jungle doesn't judge. Your group, your practice.
Do you provide marketing content?
Yes. We supply high-quality photos, video clips, and property descriptions to help you sell your retreat.
