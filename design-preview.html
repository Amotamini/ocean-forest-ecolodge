Arriving

Getting here is part of the story

Three ways in. One is our favourite.

Reach Sierpe from San José by private taxi ($70, ~6 h) or public bus ($20, ~7 h). From Sierpe, the boat glides through the mangroves to San Josecito beach — $30 at 11:30 am or $40 at 4:00 pm, about 1.5 hours. Then a 15-minute walk along the beach, and you're home.

4×4 required — unpaved roads and two shallow river crossings. Park securely at San Josecito Rural School, then the same 15-minute walk along the beach.

Fly San José → Drake Bay in about 45 minutes, then a 4×4 taxi ($70 per vehicle, ~45 min) brings you to the beach.

About

Built slowly, the way the forest builds itself

In 2000, this was an old horse pasture at the edge of the rainforest.

Year by year it became Ocean Forest: a beach yoga shala, ten rooms grown between the trees and the tide, gardens that feed the kitchen. Nothing rushed, nothing imported that the forest could provide.

Every stay feeds the same work it began with — the income supports rainforest and ocean conservation on the Osa Peninsula.
