# Ocean Forest Ecolodge — Concierge Knowledge

Edit this file whenever rates, policies, or seasonal details change. Redeploy the site to update the concierge.

## Rates & availability

Current room rates are **on request** — the concierge must not invent prices. Direct guests to complete an enquiry and the team will reply within 24 hours (usually sooner) with current rates and availability.

Rates include three garden meals daily, complimentary cacao, tea and coffee, kayaks and snorkel gear, and the daily naturalist walk.

The lodge has **ten rooms** and a maximum of **thirty guests** at any time, even on busy weeks.

## Food

Three meals a day are included. The kitchen uses fresh local Costa Rican produce and ingredients sourced from the region. Meals are omnivore — meat, fish, and plant-based options are all part of the kitchen's repertoire. Cooking is done by local women from the area, and the food reflects the place: seasonal, fresh, grounded.

Vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, and allergy accommodations are available — guests should mention dietary needs when booking.

Cacao ceremonies are available on request — enquire with the team when booking.

## Room recommendations

**Garden Bungalow** (2 guests): Best for couples who want to be close to the garden and breakfast hall. Quiet, ten metres from the kitchen. Outdoor rinse shower after the beach.

**Ocean Cabin** (2 guests): Best for couples who want Pacific views and whale-watching from the deck (June–December). On the rise above the beach.

**Lapa Family Suite** (4 guests): Best for families or small groups. Two-room suite on the second floor of the main lodge. Two queens or king plus two singles, two bathrooms, largest balcony on the property.

Every room includes en-suite bathroom with hot water, ceiling fan, memory-foam bed under mosquito netting, and private veranda or terrace with hammock.

## Daily activities & yoga

All guests have access to a daily **Budokon yoga class** held in the Dharma Hall — this is not retreat-only. Budokon is a martial arts-inspired movement practice combining yoga, mobility, and bodyweight strength. All levels welcome.

Also included for all guests: kayaks, snorkel gear, beach access, and the daily naturalist walk at 9:30am through the ethnobotanical garden.

## Excursions & adventures

The lodge organises all excursions directly — guests don't need to find their own operators. Excursion costs are not included in the room rate; each excursion is approximately **$100 per person**.

**Caño Island Biological Reserve:** One of the best snorkeling and diving spots in Costa Rica, with abundant marine life. A highlight for many guests. Bookable through the lodge.

**Corcovado National Park:** Day trips into one of the most biodiverse parks on Earth. The lodge arranges guides and logistics.

All other adventures in the area follow the same model — the team handles the planning, guests just show up. Ask about current options when enquiring or at check-in.

## Retreats

The Dharma Hall is 150 m² open-air practice space on the ground floor of Lapa Lapa Lodge. Holds up to 30 practitioners.

Retreat types hosted: Yoga & Pranayama, Cacao & Council Circles, Plants People & Place (ethnobotany), Sound Bath & Silent Retreats, Permaculture Design, Writing & Watercolour.

Upcoming retreat dates are shared by email — the concierge should not invent specific dates. Offer to capture an enquiry and the team will share the wait-list.

Whole-property buyouts available for groups of 22–30 across all ten rooms.

## Policies

**Currency:** USD and Costa Rican colones accepted. Major cards welcome.

**Cancellation:** Full refund up to 30 days before arrival. Exceptions made when life intervenes — guests should write to the team.

**Travel insurance:** Strongly recommended. The team can suggest local providers.

**Deposit:** Contact the team for current deposit requirements — do not guess amounts.

**Check-in / check-out:** Check-in after 12:00pm. Check-out before 10:00am.

**Minimum stay:** 2 nights.

**Children:** The lodge does not host children. Ocean Forest is designed for adults.

**Pets:** Not permitted.

**Solo travelers:** Very welcome. The lodge is a great fit for solo guests — the community feel, shared meals, and daily activities make it easy to connect with other guests and staff.

**Accessibility:** The lodge's remote rainforest and beach setting involves uneven terrain, boat transfers, and natural paths. It is not suited for guests with significant mobility limitations. Contact the team if you have specific questions.

## Arrival & logistics

**On arrival:** A member of the team meets every guest at the airstrip or boat dock. There is a welcome orientation and drink on arrival. The transition from travel to the lodge is looked after.

**Boat transfer from Drake Bay:** Included in the stay. The lodge handles all coastal transfers — guests don't arrange this themselves.

**If you miss the boat or arrive late:** The team will arrange drivers to get you there. Don't panic — contact the lodge directly.

**Mobile signal:** There is no mobile phone signal at the lodge. There is Wi-Fi at the main lodge building.

**Laundry:** Available on-site.

## Safety & nature

**Swimming:** San Josecito Beach is safe and beautiful for swimming. Clean open water.

**Wildlife & insects:** The Osa Peninsula is wild — insects are part of the experience. The lodge provides mosquito netting on all beds. Bring repellent. The area is home to snakes and other wildlife, but the lodge has clear protocols and guests are briefed on arrival. Thousands of visitors come through safely each year.

**Medical:** In the event of a medical need, Drake Bay village has a hospital. The team knows the local emergency procedures and will assist.

## About the lodge

Ocean Forest Ecolodge is owned and stewarded by **Jonathon**. The lodge runs on solar and micro-hydro power and is built on permaculture principles. The Lapa Lapa Lodge — the three-story main building — was constructed entirely from sustainably sourced and reclaimed materials with no living trees cut. It is an ecolodge in the full sense of the word, not a resort.

## Seasonal notes

**Dry season (roughly December–April):** Sunniest months, popular for travel. Book early.

**Green season (roughly May–November):** Afternoon rains, lush forest, fewer crowds. Mornings are often clear. Turtle nesting season runs roughly July–December on San Josecito Beach.

**Whale watching:** Humpback whales pass offshore June–December — best viewed from Ocean Cabins.

**Turtle patrols:** Guests may join night patrols or dawn hatchling releases in nesting season — enquire when booking.
