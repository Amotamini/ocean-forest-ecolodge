# Drake Bay & Osa Peninsula — Area Guide

## Location

Ocean Forest Ecolodge sits on **San Josecito Beach**, south of **Drake Bay (Agujitas)**, on Costa Rica's **Osa Peninsula** — one of the most biodiverse regions on Earth. Coordinates: 8°41'N 83°41'W.

There are **no roads** to the lodge. The last leg is always a short **coastal boat transfer** from Drake Bay village or the airstrip.

## Getting there

### From San José (SJO)

1. **Fly:** Daily ~50-minute flights from San José (SJO) to Drake Bay (DRK). The lodge meets guests at the airstrip.
2. **Boat from Sierpe:** Classic approach — ~1.5-hour boat through Central America's largest mangrove forest. Wildlife sightings common (monkeys, caimans, birds).
3. **Last mile:** From Drake Bay to the lodge — ~20-minute coastal boat transfer. The lodge handles this.

The lodge can arrange every leg of the journey — mention this when guests ask about logistics.

### Travel times (approximate)

| Leg | Duration |
|-----|----------|
| SJO → DRK (flight) | ~50 minutes |
| Sierpe → lodge (mangrove boat) | ~1.5 hours |
| Drake Bay → lodge (coastal transfer) | ~20 minutes |

Reaching the lodge from San José takes most of a morning — by design.

## Climate & seasons

**Tropical rainforest climate.** Warm year-round (typically 24–32°C / 75–90°F).

**Dry season (Dec–Apr):** Less rain, sunnier skies. Peak travel season.

**Green / rainy season (May–Nov):** Afternoon showers, vibrant forest. Mornings often clear. Lower crowds.

**Humidity:** High year-round. Light, breathable clothing recommended.

## What to pack

- Lightweight long sleeves and pants (sun + insects)
- Rain jacket or poncho (green season)
- Sturdy sandals and closed shoes for walks
- Reef-safe sunscreen and hat
- Insect repellent (natural options welcome)
- Swimsuit — beach and kayaks on-site
- Binoculars for wildlife
- Headlamp (for turtle patrols in season)
- Reusable water bottle (refill stations on-site)
- Any medications — nearest pharmacy is in Drake Bay village

Wi-Fi works at the main lodge only — kept intentionally light.

## Wildlife & nature

The Osa Peninsula hosts scarlet macaws, toucans, howler monkeys, sloths, dolphins, whales (Jun–Dec), and three species of nesting sea turtles on San Josecito Beach (olive ridley, Pacific green, rarely hawksbill).

**Daily naturalist walk:** 9:30am through the ethnobotanical garden, ends with a tasting in the kitchen. Included for all guests.

**On-site activities (included):** Kayaks, snorkel gear, daily naturalist walk, beach access.

## Nearby

**Drake Bay village (Agujitas):** Small village with basic shops, pharmacy, restaurants. Accessible by boat.

**Corcovado National Park:** One of Costa Rica's premier wilderness areas — day trips can be arranged; enquire with the lodge team.

**Sierpe:** Gateway town for the mangrove boat route; mangrove tours are a highlight of the journey itself.

## Practical tips

- Travel insurance strongly recommended
- US dollars and colones both accepted at the lodge
- Dietary restrictions — tell the lodge when booking; kitchen is flexible
- The property is off-grid (solar + micro-hydro) — power is reliable but guests should expect an ecolodge pace, not a resort
