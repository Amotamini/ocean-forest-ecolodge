Who we are

A beachfront sanctuary.
Built for practice.

In 2000 we transformed an old horse pasture into a yoga shala, right on the beach. What started as a single structure became something larger — a fully self-contained ecolodge surrounded by ethnobotanical gardens, permaculture grounds, and one of the most biodiverse forest corridors on the planet.

We've spent over two decades refining what it means to host a retreat well. That means trained staff who stay out of your way until you need them, food made by local chefs with whatever the land offers that day, and a physical space that does real work on your students before class even starts.

"We've devoted much of our income to rainforest and oceanic wildlife conservation. When you bring your group here, that's part of what they're supporting too."
Made for retreat leaders

Is Ocean Forest
right for your group?

  • You want your students fully immersed in nature — no city distractions, no competing noise
  • You're looking for a venue that handles logistics so you can focus entirely on teaching
  • Your group is between 8 and 32 people
  • You want 3 to 10 days of dedicated retreat time
  • Yoga, movement, dance, ceremony, workshops, or any combination — you bring the program, we provide the space
  • You value sustainability — our income goes directly back into rainforest conservation
  • Your participants are ready for an experience that genuinely changes them

Worth knowing

We're remote by design — 1.5 hours by small plane from San José, or a day's travel by road and boat. That remoteness is exactly what makes transformation possible here. But it does require participants who are comfortable with a real journey to get here. If your group needs easy accessibility or urban amenities nearby, we're probably not the right fit.

What you bring

Your program, your teaching, your students. That's all. We handle everything else — accommodation, three meals a day, space setup, local logistics, and any activities your group wants to add on.

The Yoga Shala

A 46-foot, three-level pagoda built entirely from reclaimed local hardwoods. The ground floor — what we call the Dharma Hall — is 1,614 square feet of tiled space, always cool even in peak heat. Good for workshops, gatherings, and ceremony. The second floor is what teachers remember: smooth polished hardwood, full of light, fully equipped with props. It has its own name here: "foot candy."

It sits right on the beach. You practice to the sound of the ocean. The jungle starts where the property ends.

the Osa waits.

Optional excursions to add depth and adventure to your retreat schedule. All with local expert guides.

Corcovado — Sirena Station

$120 per person

The most biodiverse national park on Earth. Sirena Station sits deep in primary forest — tapirs, scarlet macaws, peccaries, and all four monkey species in one walk.

Corcovado — San Pedrillo

$110 per person

A different entry point into Corcovado. Waterfall swims, coastal forest, dramatic Pacific cliffs. Slightly more accessible than Sirena.

Caño Island Snorkeling

$99 per person

Caño Island Biological Reserve. Reef fish, white-tipped sharks, sea turtles, rays — one of the best snorkeling spots in the Pacific.

Caño Island Diving

$165 per person

For certified divers. Deeper reefs, larger marine life, and visibility up to 20 meters in good conditions.

Horse Riding

$80 per person

Ride through jungle trails and along the coast with experienced local guides. Good for groups wanting something slower and grounded.
